Hoppy Blocks – free block puzzle game

Drag. Drop. Clear lines.

A relaxing block puzzle for iPhone and Android. Drag glossy jewel blocks onto an 8×8 grid, clear rows and columns, and chain streaks for big scores. Play endless Classic or hunt hidden gems across 300 Adventure levels.

The block puzzle you know, with more to do

How to play

Hoppy Blocks keeps the block puzzle rules you already know. Drag the three pieces onto the 8×8 grid, fill a whole row or column to clear it, and keep clearing to build a streak. Plan ahead: when no piece fits, the game is over. It's quick to learn, relaxing to play, and a great daily brain game.

More than a block game

Beyond endless Classic mode, Adventure turns the blocks puzzle into a journey of 300 levels: collect hidden diamonds, stars and hearts, beat score challenges, and earn three stars on every board. Turn pieces, ask for hints, and play anywhere, even offline.

Free, offline, family friendly

Hoppy Blocks is a free puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android phones. There are no accounts and no in-app purchases, ads are family-safe, and it runs without Wi-Fi, perfect for commutes, flights and quick breaks.

Questions

Is Hoppy Blocks free?

Yes. Hoppy Blocks is a free block puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android, supported by family-safe ads. There are no in-app purchases.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Your best score, Adventure stars and the game you're playing are kept even if you close the app. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection; ads simply don't load.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. There are no accounts, chats or in-app purchases. Ads are limited to G-rated, non-personalized, child-directed content, and videos play only when the player chooses to watch one.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Use the Sound and Vibration toggles on the home screen.

I found a bug or have an idea

Write to hoppyblocks.support@gmail.com. Please mention your device and whether you play on iOS or Android.
